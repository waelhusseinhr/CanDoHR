## Non-negotiable rules

1. **One write path.** Every change to business data goes through a change set (`packages/changesets`). No module writes another module's tables. No endpoint updates rows directly.
2. **Effective dating everywhere.** Never update a business row in place. Use the version helpers. Every read takes `asOf`.
3. **Money is decimal.** Use the `Money` type and `numeric(14,2)`. A JavaScript `number` holding money is a bug.
4. **Rules are data.** Statutory values, minimum wages, notice periods and agreement terms come from `rules/` and the database. Never write a tax rate, threshold or agreement term in code. If a value is missing, stop and report it. Do not guess.
5. **Payroll must match the oracle.** `tests/payroll/cases.json` is the acceptance suite, and `reference/payroll_reference.py` is the readable specification. Never edit an expected value to make a test pass. If you believe a case is wrong, stop and explain with CRA sources.
6. **The pay date decides the edition and the tax year.** The work date decides rates, agreements and cost centres.
7. **Finalized pay results are immutable.** Corrections are new results.
8. **Tenant isolation.** Every tenant table has `tenant_id` and RLS. Never connect as a role with `BYPASSRLS` from application code.
9. **Privacy.** No SIN, bank, salary, health or self-identification data in logs, events, error messages, test snapshots or AI prompts. Use the redacting logger.
10. **Screens in every screen language; data in the organization's languages.** Every user-facing string goes in the resource file of every language with screens (`en-CA` and `fr-CA` at launch). Data texts follow rule 45.
11. **Accessible.** Every screen works by keyboard and screen reader (WCAG 2.1 AA).
12. **Government formats come from the publisher.** Download CRA, Service Canada and Revenu Québec schemas, and store them under `vendor/` with source URL and date. Do not reconstruct them from memory.
13. **Quebec payroll is phase 2.** Keep Quebec fields and data structures, but `calculate()` must throw `QUEBEC_PHASE_2` for Quebec provincial tax until the owner switches it on.
14. **Humans decide.** AI features propose. Hiring, pay, discipline and termination decisions need a person.
15. **Locked runs are frozen.** A pay run reads business data with `knownAt = inputs_known_at` and rules only through its `pay_run_rule_pin` hashes. Load rules by hash and check the hash. Never edit a tested rule edition or a verified agreement version; publish a new one.
16. **One subject, one worker, one open run.** The unit of isolation is the pay subject (person + payroll account). Never split a subject across buckets, never write YTD outside finalization, and never bypass the subject lease.
17. **No guessed pay interactions.** Overtime, holiday and premium combinations come from `pay_composition` (`docs/08` §5). If a combination is not defined, raise `UNRESOLVED_INTERACTION`; never pick a default in code. `tests/agreements/stacking_cases.json` is the acceptance suite.
18. **Audit bytes are specified.** Audit leaf hashes, Merkle roots and batch hashes follow `reference/audit_merkle_reference.py` exactly and must reproduce `tests/audit/merkle_vectors.json`. Audit rows are never updated or deleted.
19. **No single-assignment assumptions.** A person can have several employments and each employment several assignments, each with its own position, agreement, rates, relationships, funding, leave plans and end date. Every manager, rate, agreement, cost centre or leave balance is read for a named assignment on a named date. `is_primary` is for headcount and display only, never for a pay, leave or benefits calculation. Which assignment governs a domain comes from `core.domain_governance` (`docs/17` §4.3).
20. **Every dollar is distributed.** Each pay result line is distributed to the cent into `payroll.pay_result_line_distribution` by the precedence in `docs/17` §8.2, using largest remainder so the parts add up exactly. Ineligible, uncovered or over-cap amounts go where the funding source's rules send them; they never disappear.
21. **Every period-based number names its calendar.** The tax year comes from the pay date. Fiscal, funder, agreement, plan, leave and pension years come from `finance.calendar`. Every GL line carries its work period and its posting period (`docs/17` §9.2).
22. **Overtime across jobs follows the jurisdiction.** The rate for an overtime hour when a person has several regular rates comes from the employment standards rule `overtime_rate_basis_multi_rate`. Never use a blended rate unless that rule says `weighted_average`. Ontario pays 1.5 × the rate for the work performed in that hour (ESA s. 22(1.1)). A jurisdiction still marked `verify_at_load` stops approval with `RULE_NOT_VERIFIED`.

## Platform rules (Supabase, Cloudflare, GitHub)

23. **Business data only through the CanDo API.** Never read or write business data through Supabase's Data API (PostgREST), GraphQL, Realtime database changes or the `supabase-js` database methods. In the browser, `supabase-js` is used for sign-in only. No CanDo schema is listed in the Data API's exposed schemas, and `anon` and `authenticated` hold no privileges on CanDo schemas.
24. **The `service_role` key stays in `apps/identity`.** It bypasses row-level security. Never bind it to another Worker, put it in a test fixture, an `.env` file, a log or a prompt, or give `apps/identity` a Hyperdrive binding.
25. **One way into the database.** Workers reach Postgres only through Hyperdrive, with query caching disabled, inside the `withTenant` wrapper (`packages/db`), as `cando_app`, or as `cando_sealer` for audit sealing only. Never cache query results in Workers KV, the Cache API or memory across requests.
26. **No personal data at the edge.** R2, Workers KV, Durable Object storage, Queue messages, Workflow step outputs, Cron payloads and logs carry identifiers, counts and hashes only. Documents and generated files go to Supabase Storage in `ca-central-1`, never R2.
27. **Design for Workers limits.** Stream large files in chunks; split long work into Queue messages or Workflow steps that can be retried safely; never rely on one invocation running long or holding a large file in memory. Every Queue consumer is idempotent and acknowledges only after its transaction commits.
28. **Migrations are append-only.** Create them with `supabase migration new`. Never edit a migration that has been merged, never run a migration against staging or production, and never put a password or key in one. GitHub Actions applies migrations.
29. **Agents get no production access.** Work against the local stack (`supabase start`, `wrangler dev`) and preview deployments with fictional data. Never ask for, use or store staging or production credentials, real personal data or AWS keys. Connect MCP servers (Supabase, Cloudflare, GitHub) only to local or development projects, with the Supabase server read-only.
30. **Work through pull requests.** One build-plan step per branch; never push to `main`, merge, or change branch protection, `CODEOWNERS`, CI workflows or `.cursor/rules/` to get a check through. Never skip, disable or weaken a failing test. Changes to protected paths (`docs/15` §Protected paths) go in their own pull request with the reason and the source.

## Code and classification rules

31. **Codes are data, never columns.** Every occupational, internal, salary survey, department, identifier or other code lives in a code scheme and reaches a job, position, incumbent or other target only through `core.code_assignment` (`docs/18` §3 and §4). Never add a column for a new kind of code, never hard-code a NOC code, TEER category or list of schemes, and read effective codes only through `core.resolve_codes()` with a date. Published classifications are loaded from `rules/classifications/` unchanged, after checking each file's SHA-256 against `schemes.json`, with their source and licence.
32. **Every code names its version.** A code from a published scheme always carries its version (`NOC-2021-1.0`, later `NOC-2026-1.0`). Never compare, convert or report codes across versions or schemes without the published correspondence in `ref.code_mapping`. A split is never resolved by a default: it becomes a decision for a person. The similarity scorer must reproduce `tests/classifications/similarity_cases.json` exactly, with `reference/position_similarity_reference.py` as the readable specification.
33. **Requirements and matches are proposals until a person decides.** NOC employment requirements are copied as `proposed` requirements with their source, never activated by code. A requirement becomes `required` only with a recorded rationale. Similar-position results and suggested codes are ranked proposals with reasons; they never use protected grounds or self-identification data, never reveal an incumbent or their pay outside the user's scope, and never override a collective agreement's posting, bumping or recall rules (rule 14).

## Compensation, evaluation and survey rules

34. **Job, market and person stay separate.** A job evaluation rates the job, market data prices the job, and a competency assessment describes a person (`docs/19` §2.1). Never compute a grade, a pay equity value or a market price from competency assessments or performance, and never change a grade from a market price. Evidence is shown beside a decision; a person decides, with a reason that is kept. Approved evaluations, published framework and plan versions, published survey editions, approved pricing runs, approved structure versions and approved survey submissions are frozen; a correction is a new version.
35. **Survey data belongs to its publisher and its licensee.** Never ship, seed, copy into tests or show in demos any real survey's data, job descriptions or level definitions; tests and demos use fictional figures. Read survey market data, survey job descriptions and pseudonyms only with the survey's access role in `app.roles`, which the database enforces (`docs/19` §12). Never estimate, impute or reverse-engineer a masked value. Survey submissions carry pseudonyms only, never names, SIN, birth dates, contact details or employee numbers, and include self-identification only with the privacy approval the schema requires.
36. **Market pricing must match the oracle.** Ageing, usable values, blending, weighting, the market line, grades, ranges, position-in-range metrics, job evaluation totals and practice comparisons follow `reference/market_pricing_reference.py` with decimal arithmetic and must reproduce `tests/compensation/market_pricing_cases.json` exactly. Never edit an expected value to make a test pass.

## People in several positions

37. **Split before you route, and never ask for "the manager".** A leave, time, overtime or availability request is split by assignment and then by approval coverage to the minute (`docs/20` §2–§3), following `reference/several_positions_reference.py` and reproducing `tests/assignments/several_positions_cases.json` exactly. Approvers are read for a named assignment, capability, date and time, never as "the employee's manager" or "the position's manager". Only the routing service writes `core.approval_part`. No one approves their own request from any of their positions; the database refuses it (`SELF_APPROVAL`) and the part goes to the approver's own approver. Pay items, one-time items, review sections, competency assessments and benefit enrolments name the assignment they belong to.
38. **Benefit changes come from the organization's rules or from HR, never from code.** When an assignment ends, each affected enrolment gets a `benefits.change_decision` from the tenant's `benefits.change_rule` rows, or goes to HR when no rule applies (`docs/20` §6.2). Never hard-code a grace period, an end-of-month rule or a reason-based outcome. The statutory floor from `benefits_during_notice` in the employment standards file comes first, and the database refuses coverage that ends before it. A decision without a rule, or with a date other than the proposed one, needs a written reason. A jurisdiction still marked `verify_at_load` raises `RULE_NOT_VERIFIED`, and `not_explicit` raises `COMMON_LAW_REVIEW`; neither is ever resolved silently.

## Core HR decisions and organization settings

39. **Settings, never constants.** Every behaviour that `docs/21` §1 lists as a setting is read with `platform.setting()` (or its API) for a date, and for the legal entity where the setting allows it. Never hard-code a default, a threshold, a hold period or a choice that is a setting, and never copy a setting into an environment variable. A new setting is a new row in `platform.setting_definition`, added by a migration with its default and the decision it comes from. Locked settings (`approvals.no_self_approval`, `approvals.hr_required`, `reporting.cross_employer`, `agreements.below_law_dual_approval`, `endings.open_case_review`) are never changeable: build no screen, import or API path that changes them, and never work around `SETTING_LOCKED`.
40. **One person, one number; positions for everyone.** The employee number lives on `core.person`, is given once by `core.next_employee_number()` and never changes; never generate or show `core.employment.employee_number` as the employee number. Every assignment fills a position (`POSITION_REQUIRED`); the line manager comes only from the position tree; never write `assignment_version.manager_assignment_id` or recreate light mode. Employment type is read per assignment, and the employment's status only through `core.employment_status_type()`. There is no gender or sex field on a person: never add one, never infer one, and never use self-identification data outside the equity programs and aggregate reports that its consent covers.
41. **Money and status changes need the right people.** Approvals for a change set come from `platform.required_approvals()`; the database refuses to approve without them (`APPROVALS_MISSING`). A negative retro or correction difference is never deducted: it becomes a held `payroll.overpayment`, and recovery items exist only after it is authorized. An employee sees a change about them only as `platform.employee_sees_change()` allows. Rehire status, its reason and its note are for HR only; recruiting reads only `core.rehire_signal()`. A bank account change follows `docs/21` §9.1 and payment files use only accounts for which `payroll.bank_account_usable()` is true. CanDo staff read a tenant's data only under an approved `platform.support_access_grant`.
42. **Agreements never pay below a law they may not replace, and job descriptions are never written by AI.** What an agreement may replace comes from `agreement_may_replace` in the employment standards file; where it may not, pay at least the law's minimum, whatever was approved. Every below-law finding an agreement may make needs approval by the HR and payroll administrators as two people before activation, and a `verify_at_load` jurisdiction blocks activation (`RULE_NOT_VERIFIED`). Never build an AI or assistant feature that drafts, rewrites or summarizes job descriptions (`docs/21` §6.3); drafts come from the NOC unit group and the job's requirements in a template. The only AI that touches them is CanDo's paid translation service, which translates and changes nothing else (`docs/22` §4.6). Never link staff of two employers without a shared-services arrangement (`CROSS_EMPLOYER_NO_ARRANGEMENT`).

## Values, fallbacks and languages

43. **Values are data.** A value an organization could want different is a setting (`platform.setting_definition`, read with `platform.setting()` for a date), a value on the organization's own list (`core.list_value`, mapped to a fixed category; logic reads only the category), or a value in a rules file with its source. Never write one in code, a database `CHECK`, a column `DEFAULT`, an API `default:`, an environment variable or a test fixture as if it were fixed. A business column that needs a default takes it from a setting through `platform.column_default`; a column with legal or pay effect has no default at all. A safety limit is a setting with a ceiling (`max_value`). A number in a requirement or story is the default of a named setting or a cited rule value, never a constant (`docs/22` §1.1).
44. **No silent fallbacks.** When a value is missing, use only a fallback in `rules/register/values-and-fallbacks.json` (`docs/22` §5), and name it in the code (`fallback: FB-NNN`). Otherwise stop with a named error (`SETTING_NOT_SET`, `LIST_VALUE_UNKNOWN`, `RULE_NOT_VERIFIED`, `JURISDICTION_RULE_MISSING`, `TRANSLATION_MISSING`, `UNRESOLVED_INTERACTION`, `INPUT_REQUIRED` and the others in `docs/22`). Never invent one: no "use 0", "use the first", "use English", "use the default profile". When a case is not covered or you are in any doubt about how a fallback should work, stop, write the question in `docs/16` with your proposal, and ask the owner; continue only with work that does not depend on it. `tests/hardcoding/check_values.py` must pass; never add a marker or a register entry just to make it pass.
45. **Screens are the user's language; words are the organization's.** Each user picks the screen language (EN or FR at launch) at the top of every screen, whatever the organization uses; never give a user a screen language that has no screens (`INTERFACE_LANGUAGE_NOT_AVAILABLE`). The organization's own words show exactly as typed, in the language it chose at setup: read every text through `platform.text_for()` with the user's screen language and show the EN or FR tag when it comes back in the other language (FB-016). Never translate an organization's words in code or with AI; translation comes only from CanDo's paid translation service (`docs/22` §4.6). An organization with a Quebec work location keeps its words in French (`FRENCH_REQUIRED_FOR_QUEBEC`). Never assume English or French in a check or a query. English and French texts stay in their columns; any other language goes in `platform.translation`, and a new version of a record carries all its texts.

## Recruitment, onboarding and probation

46. **Routes and cautions are the organization's.** Approvals for requisitions, direct hires, offers, probation extensions, conversions and caution approvals come only from the organization's routes (`platform.workflow_route`) through `platform.route_for()` and `platform.route_complete()` (`docs/23` §2.2). Never hard-code a route, an approver or a "default" route: no matching route is `ROUTE_NOT_FOUND`. When an action goes past a caution in `ref.caution`, record a `platform.caution_acknowledgement` with the text shown, and check `platform.caution_cleared()` before acting; never skip a caution, and never change or delete an acknowledgement. A new caution is a row in `ref.caution` with its wording in every screen language.
47. **People hire; the law is the floor.** Every application decision names the person who made it; AI match suggestions stay off until the organization switches them on, show their reasons and never decide (rule 14). The employee record is created only when the offer is accepted and the contract signed (`HIRE_BEFORE_CONTRACT_SIGNED`); background and criminal record checks start only after a conditional offer is made, with consent. Candidate retention, the SIN due date, pay on postings and the notice threshold come from the employment standards edition through `ref.employment_standard()`, never from code. Before probation is failed or employment ends, an HR approver reviews any open case or leave (`endings.open_case_review`, locked), and a respondent in a case never sees it. A probation whose end date passes with no decision is passed (FB-034); never leave it open.

## Payroll

48. **CanDo prepares; the employer pays; the organization's rules decide.** CanDo builds bank, remittance, slip and ROE files and reminds; it never moves pay money or files with a government. A pay run is approved only through the organization's `pay_run` route, never by its creator; failed checks hold the affected employees or stop the run as `payroll.failed_check_handling` says, and a payroll account's first live run waits for its accepted parallel run (`PARALLEL_RUN_REQUIRED`). Vacation pay, banked time and proration come from the organization's rules for each group of employees (`payroll.vacation_pay_rule_for()`, `timeleave.banked_time_policy_for()`, the pay group's method), never from code; no rule is `VACATION_PAY_RULE_MISSING` or `BANKED_TIME_POLICY_MISSING`, and the law's minimums come first. Every deduction states its shortfall rule; a garnishment is taken only after payroll confirms its first deduction; money owed is recovered only with a written authorization and never above it. Pay statements and slips are delivered only as `payroll.delivery_method()` and `payroll.may_email()` say (CRA's rule); every ROE is reviewed by HR before its file is built. An outside provider acts for a client only while `platform.provider_can()` is true, and everything it does is recorded as its own (`docs/24`).

## Compensation

49. **Pay decisions go through the organization's routes and rules.** A pay structure version, a market-based structure's philosophy, a pay decision, an incentive payout and a salary disclosure list are approved only through the organization's routes (`platform.route_complete()`), never by the person who submitted them; a market-based structure needs an approved philosophy (`PHILOSOPHY_NOT_APPROVED`). Promotions, demotions, acting pay, catch-up periods, spreads, cycle eligibility, guideline methods, exceptions, letters, proration and what people see come from the settings in `docs/25` §2.1, never from code; acting pay stops with `SETTING_NOT_SET` until the organization chooses. A pay cut on a demotion needs the caution `comp.pay_reduced_on_demotion`; a guideline exception needs a reason and, where the organization says so, HR's approval; a clawback needs the employee's signed agreement. Pay gap results use consented self-identification only and never show a group smaller than `privacy.min_group_size_self_id` (10 or more). A bargaining scenario never changes pay; only a ratified agreement version does.

## Benefits

50. **Benefit rules are the organization's; HR decides what no rule covers; the law is the floor.** Waiting periods, late enrolment, life event windows, hours tests, leave rules and coverage on termination come from each plan's setup, the organization's rules and the settings in `docs/26` §2.1, never from code; the life event window and the retro months stop with `SETTING_NOT_SET` until the organization chooses, an unpaid leave with no rule is `LEAVE_BENEFIT_RULE_MISSING`, and a position or employment ending with no rule goes to HR (FB-021). Never pick a governing agreement or an order between two plans of one kind: HR decides with a reason (`GOVERNING_AGREEMENT_NOT_SET`, FB-020, FB-023), and a carrier file leaves out anyone waiting (`COB_ORDER_NOT_SET`). Statutory leaves keep benefits as the law requires and the employee pays only in the ways the organization offers; coverage never ends before the statutory floor, and outside Ontario a decision waits for the caution `benefits.common_law_notice` with a reason. Carrier invoices are approved only through the organization's route, never by their preparer. CanDo pays no claims and stores no medical information, claim details or receipts; advisors see only what `platform.provider_can()` allows (`docs/26`).

## Time, attendance and leave

51. **Hours worked are paid; payroll never waits; the law stops with a caution.** Never drop, round away or hold back hours actually worked: rounding only as `time.punch_rounding_minutes` says (the same both ways), a break deducted only when punched or confirmed, unapproved overtime flagged (`timeleave.time_exception`) and paid, a timesheet still unapproved at the cut-off locked and paid as recorded. A rest-period or hours-limit breach stops publishing and approval until `time.rest_or_hours_limit` is acknowledged; a change to a published shift inside its notice needs `time.schedule_changed_late`. Recording methods, averaging, exemptions, leave plans (carry-over, negative limit, payout, year and tier dates) and attendance stages come from the organization's records and the settings in `docs/27` §2.1, never from code; a missing plan is `LEAVE_PLAN_MISSING` and a missing anchor `INPUT_REQUIRED`. A statutory leave is never declined for staffing or a blackout (`STATUTORY_LEAVE_NOT_REFUSABLE`); the attendance program never counts a protected absence and needs HR before discipline. No biometrics, no location coordinates, no proof of leave; managers see sensitive leaves only as "protected leave", and the team availability bar shows levels, never names or counts. No one approves their own timesheet or swap (`SELF_APPROVAL`) (`docs/27`).

## Performance

52. **People rate people, each position by itself, and the employee always sees and answers.** CanDo suggests a rating per position from the cycle's weights; the manager chooses it, with a reason when it differs (`RATING_REASON_REQUIRED`); AI never rates or ranks. A section is rated only from `reviews.min_section_days` days worked (`SECTION_TOO_SHORT_TO_RATE`), and there is no combined rating unless the organization chooses one. A review carries no rating when its agreement, its type or the hire date says so (`RATING_NOT_ALLOWED`). No review is released before calibration where the cycle uses it (`CALIBRATION_REQUIRED`), the one-up look through the organization's `performance_review` route and, for a concern the organization lists, HR's look (`HR_REVIEW_REQUIRED`); no one approves their own review or a review of themselves (`SELF_APPROVAL`). Signing means seen; a rating changed in calibration or after release names who and why and is kept. 360° results stay developmental unless the cycle says otherwise, and a group is shown only from `performance.rater_group_min` answers. An improvement plan needs its causes ruled out, HR's approval through the `pip` route and the employee's acknowledgement; never during a protected leave (`PIP_DURING_PROTECTED_LEAVE`), and a possible protected ground needs `performance.pip_protected_ground`. Weights, scales, concerns, nudges, plan length and sunset periods come from the settings in `docs/28` §2.1 and the organization's records, never from code (`docs/28`).

## Learning

53. **CanDo keeps the learning record; the law is the floor; no AI.** Never build a course player, authoring tool or AI feature in learning: courses run in the customer's LMS or a provider's platform, and suggestions come only from fixed rules with their reason shown. The training the law requires comes from `ref.training_requirement` (`rules/learning`); an organization's rule that carries it out is never looser (`TRAINING_RULE_LOOSER_THAN_LAW`), an unverified one is never applied (`RULE_NOT_VERIFIED`), and it is never waived (`LEGAL_TASK_CANNOT_BE_WAIVED`). Deadlines, reminders, refresher windows, alert days, the HR amount for requests and what happens without a repayment rule come from the settings in `docs/29` §2.1, never from code. A credential counts only once someone other than its holder verifies it; a shift is never published to someone without a valid credential the law requires (`CREDENTIAL_REQUIRED_BY_LAW`), and anything else needs `learning.not_ready_for_work`. Mandatory training outside scheduled hours is paid. Training requests are approved only through the organization's route, never by the employee; repayment follows only the organization's rules, with an agreement signed first and recovery only with written authorization. Skills are shown for internal opportunities only with the employee's consent (`docs/29`).

## Succession and workforce planning

54. **People decide; succession data stays with the people named.** Never build a flight risk model, an AI potential score or an AI successor ranking: flight risk is the manager's note, and suggestions come only from fixed rules with their reasons. Talent reviews, the nine-box and workforce planning work only once the organization switches them on (`FEATURE_OFF`). Potential, placements, successor lists and continuity sheets are shown only to the people named on the review or plan and HR (`talent.named_on()`), and every view is written to `talent.succession_view_log`; age reaches planners only in totals, and a vacancy date never carries its reason. Weights, criteria, box names, review cycles and the thin-history limits come from the settings in `docs/30` §2.1, never from code. Plans, pool memberships and the annual position plan are approved only through the organization's routes, never by their owner, nominator or nominee; a position the law requires needs an emergency successor; a unionized role's plan is for development only. Continuity sheets never hold passwords or secrets. A gap strategy only drafts an action for its own module, and a reduction in a unionized group needs labour relations and `workforce.reduction_unionized` (`docs/30`).

## Engagement and recognition

55. **People speak without being identified; recognition belongs to the person recognized.** Never store a person link or a time of day with survey answers, never show who did not answer, and never show a result for a group under `engagement.min_group_size` (or `privacy.min_group_size_self_id` for self-identified groups). An employer covered by a freedom-of-information law runs anonymous surveys only (`SURVEY_MODE_NOT_ALLOWED`). Open comments are read by HR; managers see themes; a flagged comment goes to the named contact without identity. Recognition starts private and only its recipient makes it public, through the organization's route, or attaches it to a review (`RECIPIENT_CONSENT_REQUIRED`). Every gift and award goes through `talent.reward` so CRA's limits apply, and payroll confirms the taxable part; cash awards go through the organization's route. SMS is used only for urgent alerts (`SMS_URGENT_ONLY`). Thresholds, reminder days, milestone years and the default mode come from the settings in `docs/31` §2.1, never from code (`docs/31`).

## Definition of done

See `docs/14-testing-and-quality.md` §3. A story is not done until its acceptance criteria are automated, its strings exist in every screen language, `tests/hardcoding/check_values.py` passes, permissions are tested both ways, audit entries are written, `asOf` reads are correct for past, today and future, the platform guards pass, and a human has approved the pull request.
