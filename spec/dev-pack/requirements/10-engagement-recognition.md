## Overview and scope

> **Draft status:** new specification. No source file covers engagement; the only source references are the recognition and spot-award elements of the performance workflow diagram and the service anniversary data in Core HR. Everything here is new drafting for review.

Engagement & Recognition gives employees a voice, helps leaders act on what they hear, and makes appreciation visible.

**In scope:** engagement, pulse and lifecycle surveys; confidential results and analytics; action planning; peer and manager recognition; awards and service milestones; announcements and two-way communication; wellbeing and inclusion programs (a wellbeing hub and an observances calendar at launch; employee resource groups later).

**Out of scope:** performance feedback and 360° reviews (07, which shares the recognition feed); spot award payment (03); harassment complaints and whistleblowing intake (13, though the speak-up channel links there and is available at launch).

**Positioning for SMEs and non-profits:** ready-made survey templates and simple recognition that work without a dedicated engagement team, included in every plan; larger employers can configure everything.

## Surveys

| Survey type | When | Typical content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engagement census | Annually or every two years | Engagement index, drivers (leadership, manager, growth, recognition, workload, belonging, pay fairness), open comments |
| Pulse | Monthly or quarterly | 5–15 questions tracking key drivers and recent changes |
| Lifecycle | Triggered by events, all on by default | Onboarding at 30 and 90 days (linked to Module 02), post-promotion, return from leave, exit (linked to Module 11) |
| eNPS | With pulses or alone | Likelihood to recommend the organization as a place to work |
| Inclusion | Annually | Belonging, fairness and psychological safety, analysable by self-identified group from 10 people |
| Ad hoc | Any time | Event feedback, policy consultation, return-to-office preferences |

**Survey builder:** CanDo's own tool, with a question library of validated items; Likert, multiple choice, ranking and open text; English and French versions with side-by-side translation; logic and branching; accessibility (screen reader support, plain language). Organizations can also upload a partner's or their own questionnaire and import results from another survey tool, checked against the same minimum groups.

**Distribution:** email and the mobile app at launch; reminders; response tracking by unit without identifying non-respondents.

**Confidentiality:** confidential by default (linked to HR attributes for analysis, never shown individually) with a fully anonymous option; employers covered by a freedom-of-information law run anonymous surveys only; minimum group size for any result 5 (configurable, never below 3), and 10 for self-identified groups; answers kept with the date, not the time; third-party hosting option for customers who want independence.

## Action planning

**Results for leaders:** each manager whose team meets the minimum group size gets a results page with scores, trend, comparison to the organization, top strengths and top opportunities, plus a roll-up of everyone under them, and comment themes (from keyword rules, or summarised by AI once the organization switches it on). Original comments are read by HR only.

**Team discussion kit:** guidance for sharing results with the team, discussion questions, and a template for agreeing two or three actions.

**Action plans:** expected from each manager with results within 60 days (editable), with reminders; actions with owner, due date, linked survey items and status; organization-wide actions owned by executives; progress visible to employees ("you said, we did"); next pulse measures whether things moved.

**Escalation:** comments that suggest harassment, safety risks or self-harm are flagged by keyword rules to an HR contact the organization names, with the comment's text and never the writer's identity; survey confidentiality terms tell employees this can happen, and every survey shows the speak-up channel and crisis lines.

## Recognition, awards and service milestones

**Peer and manager recognition:** short messages tied to organizational values; private by default (the recipient and their manager), made public only by the recipient and after HR approves through the organization's route; reactions and comments; mobile and Microsoft Teams or Slack posting; shared with Module 07 only when the recipient attaches it to their review.

**Points and rewards:** points budgets for managers and peers tracked in CanDo; the organization hands out rewards itself and records them (no rewards catalogue at launch); budget tracking by department.

**Formal awards:** nomination programs (for example annual excellence awards) with criteria, nomination forms, judging panels and announcements, in a later release.

**Service milestones and life events:** anniversaries from continuous service dates in Core HR; milestones every 5 years (editable); manager reminders 30 days before; certificates and gifts; birthdays and other celebrations only with the employee's opt-in.

**Tax treatment** (passed to Payroll, which confirms it): cash and near-cash awards are taxable; a gift card counts as non-cash only on CRA's conditions (single retailer or named group, cannot be converted to cash, logged); non-cash gifts and awards are non-taxable up to $500 a year, with a separate $500 for long-service awards for 5 or more years at least 5 years apart; performance rewards are taxable; the system tracks each employee's yearly totals and sends the taxable amount to Payroll. Cash awards go through the organization's approval route.

**Fairness:** recognition distribution reports by team, location, shift and self-identified group (from 10) help leaders spot people who are overlooked (for example night-shift or remote workers).

## Communication, announcements and employee voice

**Announcements:** targeted by entity, location, department, union or language; scheduled publishing; read receipts for important notices (policy changes, safety alerts) with reminders on days 3 and 7 (editable); push to mobile and to Teams or Slack; English and French versions with the employee's preferred language shown first.

**Urgent notifications:** emergency alerts (site closures, weather, safety incidents) by push, email and SMS to everyone at a location, including staff without corporate email; SMS is used only for these alerts.

**Employee voice:** idea submission with voting and status tracking; always-on anonymous feedback box with two-way anonymous replies; Q&A for town halls.

**Speak-up channel:** a separate confidential channel at launch for harassment, safety, ethics and fraud reports, apart from engagement feedback, so concerns get proper handling; designed in the Module 13 review.

**Directory and profiles:** people directory with name, role, unit and work contact; photo, skills, interests and name pronunciation only if the employee adds them; org chart from Module 01.

## Wellbeing and inclusion programs

**Wellbeing:** a resource hub at launch linking EAP (Module 05), mental health supports, crisis lines, financial wellness and wellness account information; wellbeing challenges and events later, with voluntary participation; psychological health and safety resources aligned with the CSA Z1003 National Standard for organizations that adopt it.

**Employee resource groups:** in a later release; groups with leads, membership (voluntary and hidden by default), events, budgets and communication channels; ERG feedback channels to leadership.

**Cultural observances calendar:** a starter calendar with National Indigenous Peoples Day, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, Black History Month, Asian Heritage Month, Pride, major religious observances and others the organization chooses, with educational content and event support; none becomes a paid holiday unless the holiday calendar says so.

**Indigenous organizations:** options to reflect community events, ceremonies and land-based activities in programs and calendars.

**Boundaries:** participation is voluntary; individual wellbeing data is never visible to managers; programs do not collect health information.

## Data model, privacy and integrations

| Entity | Key fields |
| --- | --- |
| Survey | Type, questions, languages, schedule, audience, confidentiality mode, minimum group size |
| Response | Survey, answers, group attributes (confidential mode only; the unit in anonymous mode), date |
| Result Snapshot | Survey, unit, scores, n, suppression flags |
| Action Plan | Owner, unit, items, linked questions, status |
| Recognition | Giver, receiver(s), value, message, visibility, points or award amount |
| Award Program | Criteria, nominations, judges, winners (later release) |
| Milestone | Employee, type, date, gift, status |
| Announcement | Audience, content (EN/FR), schedule, read receipts |
| Idea / Feedback | Submitter (or anonymous), content, votes, status, replies |
| ERG | Name, leads, members, events, budget (later release) |

The tables are in `schema/0017_engagement_decisions.sql` (`docs/31` §11).

**Privacy design:** survey responses stored separately from the HR record, with attributes attached only at the group level for analysis; administrators cannot look up an individual's answers; anonymity thresholds enforced in every report and export; consent notices explain what is collected and how it is used, as required under PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25.

**Integrations:** Core HR (attributes, anniversaries, org); Onboarding and Offboarding (lifecycle surveys); Performance (recognition the employee attaches); Payroll (taxable awards); Benefits (EAP and wellness links); collaboration tools (Teams, Slack); an SMS gateway for urgent alerts.

## Public-sector and large-employer engagement

| Area | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Survey scale | Census surveys for tens of thousands with results for every unit above the anonymity threshold |
| Frontline reach | Announcements and urgent alerts reaching staff without corporate email (transit, public works, care, seasonal) by app and SMS; surveys by email and app at launch |
| Unions | Survey content and timing coordinated with unions where agreements require, with a caution before opening; results shared at joint committees |
| Recognition limits | Recognition budgets and tax limits applied the same way across all employers in a shared-services group |
| Access to information | Employers covered by a freedom-of-information law run anonymous surveys only, so no individual responses exist to disclose |
| Languages | English and French by default; Inuktut and other languages where required |
