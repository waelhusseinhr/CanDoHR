| 9 Pay runs | **On main** (pull request 10) | A pay date on a weekend or a bank holiday moves to the previous banking day, and a move into the prior year is flagged. | 10 Retro | **On main** (pull request 13) | A committed past change to rates or related pay inputs opens one retro request for each finalized period that differs.
